Our Commitment: "Between Us" shows no ads, sells no data, and embeds no third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking SDKs. You can use every free feature without an account; data is processed only when you choose to sign in or make a purchase.

1. What We Process

  • Account data (optional): If you create an account we store your email and a salted hash of your password (or the identifier provided by Apple / Google) to sync your purchases across devices. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Purchase history: The packs, themes and tips you bought, so they can be restored on your devices. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store; we never receive your card details.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: A randomly generated anonymous device ID, session length and platform (iOS / Android / Web), used only to understand overall usage. It contains no question or conversation content and is not linked to your account. You can turn it off any time in the app's Privacy screen.
  • Couple sync rooms: When you use Sync Room, the current question text and a 4-digit room code are held temporarily on our server so you and your partner see the same card; they expire automatically.
  • Plus cloud backup (optional): Only when you have unlocked Plus and are signed in, your favorites, conversation notes, seen-question progress and custom questions are backed up to our server, tied to your account, so they can be restored on a new device. They are never used for analytics or ads and are permanently deleted with your account.
  • Daily reminder (optional): If you turn it on, the app asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your phone. The content and time stay on your device and are never sent to a server.
  • No device permissions: The app never requests camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

2. Local Storage & Deletion

Preferences, language, favorites, your progress and conversation notes stay on your device; your sign-in credentials are kept in the system secure store (iOS Keychain).

  • Delete your account: When signed in, open the Account screen in the app and tap "Delete Account". Your account and cloud entitlements are deleted; orders keep only de-identified financial records.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all local data.
  • No sale of data: We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.
